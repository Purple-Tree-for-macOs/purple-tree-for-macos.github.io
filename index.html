Purple Tree

A visualization utility for your hard drive space usage. The tree map display reveals large folders and files at a glance, with visual size comparisons, duplicate detection, and effortless space management. Fast, beautiful, and precise.

Instant Visibility

Discover the largest files and folders on your drive. Gain a clear understanding of your disk structure through an intuitive tree map interface.

Color-Coded Insights

Identify files by content type—Pictures, Music, Movies—with distinct colors. Track recently modified files using Date Modified mapping for targeted cleanup.

Duplicate Management

Find, display, and manage duplicated files effortlessly. Reclaim space without the hassle of manual searches.

Seamless Exploration

Zoom in and out to explore subfolders. Access detailed information on selected items, with options to delete directly and free up space.

Flexible Scanning

Scan external drives, exclude unwanted locations for faster scans, and mark favorites for quick access to previous results.

How It Works

Step 1: Select Drive

Choose your internal or external drive from the startup pane. Exclude folders to optimize scan speed.

Step 2: Scan and Visualize

Run a quick scan. View the tree map with sizes, colors for file types, and recent changes highlighted.

Step 3: Explore and Manage

Zoom into folders, spot duplicates, and review details. Delete items or mark scans as favorites.

Step 4: Free Up Space

Reclaim disk space instantly. Your drive stays organized and efficient.

FAQ

Is Purple Tree compatible with macOS Sequoia?

Yes, fully optimized for the latest macOS versions, including external drive support.

How fast is the scan?

Scans complete in seconds to minutes, depending on drive size. Exclusions speed it up further.

Can I recover deleted files?

Purple Tree provides previews before deletion. Use macOS Time Machine for recovery if needed.

What about privacy?

All scans are local. No data leaves your Mac.
